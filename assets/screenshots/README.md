# Live lyrics screenshots

`lyrics-live-01.png` and `lyrics-live-02.png` were captured from the active
tiny-dfr DRM framebuffer on a T2 MacBook on September 11, 2026, during Radio
Atlas playback. They show the actual 2170 × 60 display at two different times.
The capture reverses the panel's portrait scanout orientation and converts its
pixels to PNG; the interface and text have not been recreated or retouched.

The song shown is **Right Beside You — Sophie B. Hawkins**. Artwork and brief
lyric excerpts are third-party content shown to illustrate the application's
lyrics and metadata display; the project's MIT license does not grant rights
to that content. These captures are separate from the fictional artwork in
the marketplace `preview.png`.
